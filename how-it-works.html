How It Works

Learn how simple it is to start your journey of Quran and Arabic learning from the comfort of your home.

Home / How It Works
Simple Process

3 Steps to Get Started

We have made it incredibly easy for new students to begin their learning journey.

1

Book a Free Trial

Fill out the simple form on our website. Our team will contact you via WhatsApp to schedule a time that suits you.

2

Attend Trial Class

Meet your teacher in a 30-minute live session. We will assess your level and create a custom study plan for you.

3

Start Regular Classes

If you are satisfied, choose a monthly plan and schedule. Complete the registration and start your official classes!

Requirements

What You Need To Start

You don't need any complex setup or expensive equipment to learn with us. As long as you have the basics, you are ready to go!

A Device

A laptop, tablet, or even a smartphone with a working camera and microphone.

Internet Connection

A stable internet connection sufficient for video calls.

Zoom or Skype

We use these free apps for our live interactive virtual classrooms.

Need Technical Help?

Our support team can help you set up Zoom and test your microphone before your first class.

The Classroom

The 1-on-1 Experience

What does a typical class look like? Here is a breakdown.

Live Interaction

It's not recorded videos. You and your teacher are live, sharing screens, using digital boards, and reading together.

Full Attention

Because it is 1-on-1, the teacher focuses solely on your pronunciation and pace, ensuring faster progress.

Progress Reports

Parents receive monthly reports detailing what Surahs were covered and areas that need practice.
