Dedicated to Authentic Learning

Student Journey

Your Path to Mastery

A typical progression for a student starting from the basics.

1

Foundation (Months 1-3)

Mastering Noorani Qaida, recognizing letters, correct pronunciation (Makhraj), and basic connection rules.

2

Fluency (Months 4-9)

Transitioning to reading the full Quran Mushaf. Focusing on smooth, continuous reading without spelling out letters.

3

Tajweed Application (Months 10-15)

Learning and applying the advanced rules of Tajweed (Noon Sakinah, Meem Sakinah, Madd rules) for beautiful recitation.

4

Memorization & Deepening (Ongoing)

Starting the Hifz journey and learning basic Arabic grammar to understand the meaning of the verses.
