Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our courses, timings, and platform.

Home / FAQ

Course Information

Do you offer courses for absolute beginners?
Yes, absolutely. Our "Noorani Qaida" course is specifically designed for absolute beginners of all ages. You do not need to know any Arabic to start.
What is the duration of the courses?
The duration varies depending on the course and the student's learning pace. Typically, the Qaida course takes 3-4 months, and the Quran reading course takes 6-8 months.

Class Timing

Can I choose my own class schedule?
Yes, you can select your preferred days and times. We operate 24/7 and have teachers available across all global time zones.

Fees & Payment

How do I pay for the classes?
We accept online payments via Credit/Debit Cards, PayPal, and Bank Transfers. You will receive an invoice at the beginning of each month.

Teacher Related Questions

Can female students request a female teacher?
Yes, we have highly qualified female teachers available for sisters and young girls to ensure complete comfort and privacy.

Technical Support

What software do I need for the classes?
We primarily use Zoom, Skype, or our dedicated interactive portal. You only need a stable internet connection and a device with a microphone and camera.

Still Have Questions?

Feel free to contact us directly via WhatsApp or our contact form.
