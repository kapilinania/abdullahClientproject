Privacy & Terms

Please read our policies carefully to understand how we operate and protect your data.

Home / Privacy & Terms

1. Privacy Policy

At Abdullah Quran Academy, we are committed to protecting the privacy of our students and visitors. We only collect the personal information necessary to provide our educational services, such as your name, email address, phone number, and student age. We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

2. Terms & Conditions

By accessing our website or enrolling in our courses, you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms and Conditions. Our services are provided on a subscription basis, and fees are payable in advance at the beginning of each month. We reserve the right to modify or terminate services if these terms are violated.

3. Refund Policy

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee if you are not satisfied with our services after your first paid week. To request a refund, please contact our support team via email. Refunds after the 7-day period are processed on a case-by-case basis and may be prorated.

4. Student Conduct Policy

Students are expected to maintain proper Adab (etiquette) during live sessions. This includes being punctual, having a working camera and microphone, dressing modestly, and speaking respectfully to the teacher. Disruptive behavior may result in suspension from classes without a refund.

5. Data Protection Information

We use secure servers and encryption to protect all transmitted data. Live classes may be recorded strictly for quality assurance and teacher training purposes only; these recordings are stored securely and are never published publicly.
